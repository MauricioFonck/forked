# Code of Conduct

FORKED adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be welcoming and respectful, critique ideas rather than
people, give credit, and assume good faith.

Specific to this project:

- Do not fabricate results, benchmarks, users, endorsements or partnerships.
- Distinguish clearly between proposal, implementation and validated result.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/MauricioFonck/forked/security/advisories/new)
(marked "conduct") until a dedicated contact exists. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.

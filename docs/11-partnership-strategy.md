# 11. Research, Partnership, and Sponsorship Strategy

## 11.1 Objective
Seek technical review, integrations, compute credits, research collaboration, and eventually financial support. A sponsor must not be presented as a partner until a formal agreement exists.

## 11.2 Partner value proposition
FORKED can offer:
- a public testbed for replay and experiment integrity;
- reproducible benchmark cases;
- integration adapters for diverse ecosystems;
- a neutral, open artifact format;
- transparent research results;
- opportunities for upstream contributions.

## 11.3 Engagement sequence
1. Publish a technically credible alpha.
2. Publish a benchmark with reproducible scripts.
3. Obtain independent review from maintainers/researchers.
4. Open integration proposals.
5. Contact relevant open-source programs and research groups with a concise evidence-based proposal.
6. Request a specific contribution: review, integration, compute credits, or research collaboration.
7. Publish any accepted collaboration accurately.

## 11.4 Target stakeholder categories
- AI model providers and agent platform teams.
- Workflow orchestration projects.
- AI observability and evaluation vendors.
- Cloud and compute providers.
- Universities and research labs.
- Open-source foundations and developer communities.

## 11.5 Tailored collaboration themes
- Model providers: adapter compatibility and reproducibility of model/tool workflows.
- Observability projects: trace-to-experiment interoperability.
- Workflow engines: checkpoint and replay adapter semantics.
- Universities: benchmark methodology, causal interpretation, independent evaluation.
- Infrastructure vendors: isolated execution and artifact portability.

## 11.6 Anthropic-specific path
Anthropic's Claude for Open Source program has published eligibility criteria and offers Claude Max access for approved maintainers/contributors. Eligibility and program terms can change. Review the official page immediately before applying. Do not assume a new repository automatically qualifies or that six months of access is guaranteed.

Official program: https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss

A strong application should provide:
- public repository and active maintenance evidence;
- clear project impact and users;
- contribution history;
- adoption metrics that can be verified;
- a concrete explanation of how Claude supports open-source maintenance;
- transparent roadmap and governance.

## 11.7 Sponsor package
Prepare:
- 2-page executive brief;
- technical architecture;
- demo video;
- benchmark report;
- public roadmap;
- security/privacy statement;
- contributor and adoption metrics;
- specific support request and budget.

## 11.8 Ethical communications
Never fabricate users, downloads, partners, endorsements, benchmarks, or research results. Do not imply endorsement by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, or any other organization without permission.

## Notes
- The competitor review is qualitative and scoped. It is not a complete market map.
- Phoenix is a strong adjacent product with tracing, evaluation, datasets, experiments, and span replay; FORKED must differentiate beyond those capabilities.
- LangGraph supports checkpoint-based time travel and branching.
- Temporal supports durable workflow replay with determinism constraints and recorded external activity results.
- OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions are an interoperability opportunity, not a competitor to a runtime.
- No claim of patent novelty, freedom to operate, or guaranteed market gap is made.
- A formal prior-art and legal review is required before public claims of originality.

# Governance

## Current stage: founder-led

FORKED is an early project. The project lead maintains scope, reviews changes and
coordinates releases. We do not claim community governance until maintainers and
processes exist (see [`docs/10-governance-and-open-source.md`](docs/10-governance-and-open-source.md)).

## How decisions are made

- Technical decisions are recorded as ADRs. The founding one is
  [`docs/14-adr-001-hexagonal-architecture.md`](docs/14-adr-001-hexagonal-architecture.md).
- Protocol (CIP) and schema changes require a written proposal with a migration plan.
- Anyone can propose a change through an issue; scope is defended by the roadmap in
  [`docs/09-roadmap-and-backlog.md`](docs/09-roadmap-and-backlog.md): no P2/P3 work while
  P0/P1 acceptance criteria fail.
